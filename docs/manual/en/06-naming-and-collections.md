# Product names and collections

## GEN-03 Product names

A new product's name follows the pattern **`<Evocative name> <Family>`**:

- in English, in Title Case;
- **2–4 words in total**, counting the family word;
- the last word is the family name: Round, Faceted, Lentil, Rondelle, Crescent, Initial or
  Disc;
- **unique in the catalog**, ignoring upper and lower case. A name of a discontinued
  product is not reused.

Examples: *Desert Sand Faceted* (GB-1002), *Deep Forest Lentil* (GB-1033), *Sea Moon
Crescent*.

Products named before this edition keep their names (for example *Terracotta Sunrise* or
*Crescent Moon & Star*).

*Why:* the family word tells customers and the warehouse what the product is, and unique
names avoid wrong picks.

*Violation example:* "Midnight Lagoon Round" already exists (GB-1001). A different name such
as "Midnight Tide Round" is needed.

## Collections

Every product belongs to one collection. The palettes are guidance for the designer, not
rules.

| Collection | Idea | Suggested colors and finishes |
|---|---|---|
| **Permanente** | The core line, sold all year | Any approved color or finish |
| **Coastal Garden** | The sea and the garden: cool, fresh tones | Aqua, Sea Green, Cobalt, Emerald, Clear; rhodium |
| **Amber Hollow** | Autumn in the woods: warm, glowing tones | Amber, Honey, Terracotta, Desert Sand, Deep Forest; antique bronze, gold plate |

## Catalog text

The catalog description is 1–2 sentences, warm and concrete: it names the shape, size,
color or finish and the collection, and does not promise anything the product does not do
(for example "lucky" or "healing"). Bead Studio's assistant drafts it; the designer approves
it.
